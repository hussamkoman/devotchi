Devotchi pets

A pet is data: its own palette, body parts, face style and four auxiliary channels, posed by the shared channel table of the firmware rig (pet_pose.c). Everything is rounded rectangles, circles, short polylines and a few triangles. See pet-animations.md.

Devo, the signature pet: 11 moods

A soft square with an antenna, and the pet that changes colour with its mood. The antenna is its second face: it wags, droops, kinks and curls, and its tip is the on-screen twin of the device LED. The four everyday moods (first four tiles) are warm and friendly, and each has its own cue that survives without colour.

Ember, the alternative pet: 11 moods

A small jade dragon: big head on a small body, snout with nostrils and fangs, slit pupils, lids instead of brows, orange wings, ear fins and tail spade. Ember keeps its jade in every mood (a mood only shifts it lighter, darker or paler); wings, tail, smoke and fire carry the feeling.
