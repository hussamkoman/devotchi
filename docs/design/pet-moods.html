Devotchi pets

A pet is data: its own palette, body parts, face style and four auxiliary channels, posed by the shared channel table of the firmware rig (pet_pose.c). Everything is rounded rectangles, circles, short polylines and a few triangles. See pet-animations.md.

Devo, the signature pet: 11 moods

The ghost from the PCB silkscreen: a dome, straight sides and a three-bump hem, with the antenna and ball of the small logo. It has the board's look: a dark body with a white outline and white circuit traces, vias and chips, round eyes with a glint and a small smile. The body and the traces never change: the mood colour shows on the antenna ball and on the light in the big chip (grey asleep), with the face and the antenna. It floats over a soft shadow while its hem ripples; the light in the big chip pulses while it thinks, flickers when anxious and is off when sad or asleep, and the antenna ball is the on-screen twin of the device LED. Drawn here from its pack by the dashboard's renderer.

Mochi: 11 moods

A soft square with an antenna, in one apricot hue that a mood only shifts lighter, darker or paler. The antenna is its second face: it wags, droops, kinks and curls.

Mochi: reactions

Ember, the alternative pet: 11 moods

A small jade dragon: big head on a small body, snout with nostrils and fangs, slit pupils, lids instead of brows, orange wings, ear fins and tail spade. Ember keeps its jade in every mood (a mood only shifts it lighter, darker or paler); wings, tail, smoke and fire carry the feeling.

Ember: reactions

Sprout, a catalog pet: 11 moods

An egg with a sprouting leaf, sent to a device from the dashboard rather than compiled in. One pink hue shifted by mood; the leaf tilts and droops with the feeling. Drawn from its pack by the dashboard's renderer.
